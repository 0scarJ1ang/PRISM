**Better policies, better understanding, better composition.**

I work on robot learning with three connected goals: learning better policies, understanding where and why they work, and composing them to solve new tasks. I am interested in how structure and guidance can make learned skills more adaptable and reusable beyond their training demonstrations.

My work on GSSIP studies how to steer generative policies during execution without retraining. In APPL, I use structural priors to connect policy learning with an agent's understanding and composition of skills.
